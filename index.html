FixIndex is a prototype that adapts the published scoring model of the Yuka app (weighted criteria, hazard caps, same-category alternatives) to consumer electronics repairability. Yuka is not affiliated with this prototype. Methodology sources: Yuka presskit, Wired, FoodNavigator.

How the score works

Every product is graded on five criteria, each scored 0–100. The final index is their weighted sum, capped by serious repair barriers — the same shape as Yuka's published food model (60% nutrition, 30% additives, 10% organic, with hazard caps), adapted for repairability.

Scoring criteria and weights
CriterionWeightWhat it measures
Repair documentation25%Free guides, teardowns and schematics
Spare parts availability25%Official and third-party parts, fairly priced
Ease of disassembly20%Standard fasteners, modular design, little adhesive
Battery serviceability15%Replacing the battery without special tools or risk
Software support15%Pledged years of OS and security updates ÷ 7

Barrier caps

A severe barrier (e.g. serialized parts that stop working after replacement) caps the score at 24. A major barrier (e.g. a battery glued into the frame) caps it at 49. Minor flaws appear as amber risk cards without capping.

Badges

  • 70–100 — Repair-friendly
  • 40–69 — Mixed repairability
  • 0–39 — Repair-hostile

Alternatives

When a product scores below 60, FixIndex suggests up to three higher-scoring products in the same subcategory — independent picks, ordered by score.

Provenance: documentation-based adaptation. No Yuka code or data was reverse-engineered; only its publicly described methodology was used.
